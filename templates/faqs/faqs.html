Frequently asked questions

Answers about our services, how projects work, timelines, technology and support.

What does Xvera Labs do?

We design and build custom software, web and mobile applications, e-commerce platforms, AI-powered features and cloud systems. We also offer UI/UX design, DevOps, quality assurance and technology consulting.

Do you build custom software solutions?

Yes. Every solution is shaped around your requirements, workflows and growth plans, rather than forced into a ready-made template.

Do you work with startups?

Yes. We help startups plan and build MVPs, test ideas, launch products and scale them as the business grows.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in different countries and regions, using video calls and email, and we agree meeting times that suit both sides.

How do I start a project with you?

Send us your requirements through the contact form. We review them, arrange a call, and then share a plan with scope, timeline and cost, so you can decide with the full picture.

How long does a project usually take?

It depends on the size and complexity. Small projects can take a few weeks, while larger platforms may take several months. We give you a realistic timeline after we understand what you need.

How is a project priced?

We price after a discovery conversation, based on scope, complexity and timeline. You receive a clear estimate before any work starts, so you know what to expect.

Can I follow progress during the project?

Yes. We work in short iterations with regular demos, so you can see real progress, give feedback and adjust priorities as you go.

Which technologies do you use?

We work with Django, Laravel, React, Next.js, Flutter, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS and modern cloud-native tools. We pick the stack that fits your product, not the other way round.

Do you build AI features and chatbots?

Yes. We integrate AI providers such as OpenAI, Claude and Gemini into products, and build chatbots, knowledge assistants and workflow automation connected to your own data.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?

Yes. We can modernize an older product with a better design, faster performance, stronger security and responsive layouts, while keeping what already works.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, monitoring, updates, security fixes and performance improvements, so your product stays healthy after it goes live.

Is my information safe when I contact you?

We only use what you send us to reply and plan work with you. Read our Privacy Policy to see what we collect, why, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Can I partner with or invest in Xvera Labs?

Yes. We have referral, reseller, marketing, strategic and investor programs, each under a written agreement. See the partner programs or apply directly.

Are you hiring?

Check our careers page for open roles and ways to join the team.
