The short version

  • We collect what you send us (for example in the contact form, the chat or a partner application) and a few technical details such as your IP address.
  • We use it to reply to you, plan work with you, run partner programs and keep the site safe.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time. See Your rights.

Who we are

This website is run by Xvera Labs, a software company based at 297-C, P.I.A. Main Boulevard Road, Lahore, Pakistan. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Xvera Labs. We decide how and why the personal information described here is used.

Information we collect

What you give us

  • Contact form and chat: your name, email, phone number, company name and website, region, the topic and services you are interested in, an optional budget range, and the project details you write.
  • Partner applications: the type of partnership, your name, email, phone number, company, region, your message, how you heard about us and, for investor partners, the module and approximate investment range you choose.
  • Other contact: anything you send by email, phone or WhatsApp.

What we collect automatically

  • Your IP address and the time you submit a form, which we use to prevent spam and abuse.
  • The page you were on when you opened the chat.
  • Basic technical data in our hosting and server logs, such as browser type and pages requested.

Please do not send us sensitive information such as passwords, payment card numbers or government ID numbers through our forms.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message, arrange calls and prepare proposals.
  • To review partner applications, and to prepare partner or investor agreements.
  • To send a confirmation of your enquiry or application.
  • To keep our website secure and to stop spam and abuse.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Why we may process it

Depending on where you live, the law may require us to have a reason for using your information. Ours are:

  • Your request: to answer your enquiry or take steps you ask for before we agree any work.
  • Consent: where you tick the box on a form to let us contact you. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to run, secure and improve our business, where this does not override your rights.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or share certain records.

Cookies and storage

We keep this to what the site needs to work.

  • Cookies: a security cookie that protects our forms from forgery (CSRF), and a session cookie that lets the site remember you between pages.
  • Browser storage: your browser's session storage remembers that you have seen the loading screen, and keeps an unsent draft of the chat form. Both are cleared when you close the tab.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site, such as forms, may stop working.

Who we share it with

Only our team can see the enquiries and applications you send. We share information with others only when needed:

  • Service providers who help us run the site, such as web hosting and email delivery.
  • Content providers whose files our pages load, such as Google Fonts and the Font Awesome icon library. When your browser loads them, they can see your IP address.
  • Google Search Console, which we use to monitor how our site appears in search.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is ever sold or merged. We would tell you if this changed how your data is used.

International transfers

Our team works from Pakistan, and some of our providers may store or process data in other countries. Where we can, we choose providers that protect personal information to a reasonable standard.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and applications only as long as we need them to reply, to work with you, and to keep proper business and legal records. After that we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your information earlier, as set out below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Our site is served over HTTPS, access to submitted enquiries is limited to authorised team members, and our forms use anti-spam protection. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us at xveralabs@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on a request.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Our site may link to other websites, such as WhatsApp or social media. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will make it clear on our site.
