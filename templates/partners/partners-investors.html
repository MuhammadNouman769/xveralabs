Profit sharing

How profit sharing works

Each module shares a portion of its net profit with its investor partners. The numbers are written into your agreement, not left to a handshake.

{{ terms.investor_share|default:"5%" }}indicative share of a module's net profit*
  • Net profit means what is left after the module's costs, as defined in the agreement.
  • Your capital, share and term are set per module and written down before you commit.
  • Reports show the module's results so you can see how your share was calculated.

* Indicative rate for the program. The exact share is set per module in your written agreement. It is not a promised return.

FAQ

Investor questions

Are returns guaranteed?

No. A share of profit depends on how the module performs. There may be periods with low or no profit, and capital is at risk.

Can I choose which module to invest in?

Yes. You choose the module that interests you, and the agreement is specific to that module.

What is the minimum amount?

The amount is set per module and shared in the investor pack. Tell us your approximate range in the application and we will point you to the right option.

Should I involve a lawyer or advisor?

We recommend it. A written agreement is a serious commitment, and your own advisor can review it for you before you sign.

How do I apply?

Fill in the short application form and select "Investor partner". We will contact you to arrange an intro call.

Interested in a module?

Apply to receive the investor pack. There is no commitment until you sign an agreement.
