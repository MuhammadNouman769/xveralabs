FAQ

Questions partners ask

Who can become a partner?

Individuals, agencies and companies. Choose the program that fits you in the application form, and we will tell you if another one suits you better.

Do I need a registered company?

It depends on the program. Referral and marketing partners can often start as individuals. Reseller, strategic and investor partnerships may need a registered business. We will confirm this during the intro call.

When and how is the commission paid?

Commission is paid after we receive the client's payment, as set out in your agreement. The exact basis and timing are written down before you start.

Is there a written agreement?

Yes. Every partnership starts with a written agreement that covers scope, rewards and responsibilities. We recommend reading it carefully and getting your own advice if you need it.

Are investor returns guaranteed?

No. A share of profit depends on how the product module performs, and capital is at risk. Please read the investor page for details.
