What is AI consulting, and what's included? +

What business problems and use cases can AI consulting solve? +
Common use cases include workflow automation, intelligent document processing, customer service augmentation, predictive analytics, quality control, supply chain optimization, and decision support systems.
How much do AI consulting services cost, and what determines the price? +
Cost depends on scope, complexity, data readiness, and the level of implementation support required. We offer flexible engagement models tailored to your budget and goals.
How do we choose the right AI consulting partner, and what should we look for? +
Look for proven experience in your industry, a business-outcome focus, strong governance and security practices, and the ability to support you from strategy through implementation and scale.
Which industries and company sizes do you work with? +
We work with enterprises across healthcare, finance, retail, logistics, and technology, from mid-market organizations to Fortune 500 companies.
Do you build custom AI solutions, agents, and copilots, or only advise? +
We do both. We provide strategic consulting and also design, build, and deploy custom AI solutions, agents, and copilots tailored to your business.
How long does a typical AI engagement take before we see results? +
Initial assessments and roadmaps typically take 4-8 weeks. Quick-win implementations can show results within 8-12 weeks, with full-scale transformations spanning 6-12 months.
What engagement models do you offer? +
We offer project-based, retainer, and managed services engagement models, as well as embedded team augmentation.
Do we need in-house AI experts? +
Not necessarily. We can supplement your existing team or provide end-to-end expertise, and we help build internal capability through enablement and training.
How do you ensure data security? +
We follow industry-standard security frameworks, implement strict data governance, and ensure compliance with regulations like GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2.
How do you measure success? +
We define success metrics upfront tied to your business KPIs, such as cost reduction, revenue growth, efficiency gains, and risk mitigation, and track them throughout the engagement.
