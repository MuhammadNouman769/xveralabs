Every hand-off between companies loses context, adds delay and makes it unclear who is responsible when something breaks. Keeping strategy, design, engineering, testing and operations together removes those gaps.

03

Agile delivery

Work is organised in short cycles, so you see real progress often and can change direction early, when it is cheap to do so.

Requirements always change once real users get involved. Agile delivery accepts that, and turns it into an advantage: you learn quickly, fix early and spend your budget on what matters.

  1. Plan

    Pick the most valuable work for the next sprint, together with you.

  2. Build

    Engineers deliver small, tested pieces of the product.

  3. Review

    You see a working demo and give feedback.

  4. Improve

    We adjust the plan and the process, then repeat.

?

Questions we are often asked

How do we get started?

Send us a short description through the contact form. We reply to set up a call, ask questions about your goals and users, and then propose a scope, a team and a first milestone.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP can take weeks, while a larger platform is delivered in phases over several months. After discovery we give you a timeline with the reasoning behind it.

How is pricing decided?

We estimate after discovery, once the scope is clear. Depending on the project we can work on a fixed scope, with a dedicated team, or in a short MVP sprint.

Who owns the code and the data?

Ownership is set out in our written agreement. For custom work, the code you pay for is yours, and your data stays yours.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before you share any details about your project.

What happens after launch?

We can monitor, maintain and improve the product after go-live, including bug fixes, updates and new features, or hand over cleanly to your own team with documentation.
