How we work

A clear process, and a model that fits you

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, users and limits, then agree on scope, timeline and cost.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes and a clickable prototype, so you see the product before we build it.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short iterations with regular demos, so you can steer the product as it grows.

  4. 04

    Launch and support

    Deployment, monitoring and ongoing maintenance after go-live.

Project delivery
