About the program

Why become an ambassador?

As a Campus Ambassador you are the friendly face of Xvera Labs on your campus. You help students learn about technology and opportunities, organise activities with our support, and build skills in communication, event planning and leadership.

It is a way to gain real experience, meet people and show initiative, long before you graduate.
