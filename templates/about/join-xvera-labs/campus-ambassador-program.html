Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying.

Who can apply for the program?

Any currently enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student in India with a valid college ID can apply. We welcome students from all streams — engineering, business, design, arts, and sciences.

Is there any application fee?

No, applying is completely free. We never charge ambassadors for participation, training, or kits.

How much time do I need to commit?

Expect 5–8 hours per week. This includes event hosting, content creation, and attending bi-weekly virtual meetups.

Is this a paid role?

It's a volunteer program with performance-based rewards. Top ambassadors earn cash prizes, vouchers, and priority internship offers.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every ambassador who completes the 3-month term receives an official certificate and a LinkedIn recommendation letter.

Can I apply if I'm in my first year?

Absolutely! We love first-year energy. Around 30% of our ambassadors are in their first year of college.
