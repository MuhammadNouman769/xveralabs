Employee success

We succeed when you do. Here is how we try to help every person do the best work of their career.

  1. S1

    A proper welcome

    Onboarding that introduces the team, the tools and what good work looks like, so your first weeks feel clear instead of confusing.

  2. S2

    Mentorship and feedback

    Code reviews, pairing and regular conversations with people who have done the work before, in a culture where questions are welcome.

  3. S3

    Room to learn

    Support for building new skills that matter to your role, on real projects and beyond them. {# EDIT: add your learning budget or courses if you have them #}

  4. S4

    A clear path forward

    You should always know what the next level looks like and what it takes to get there, and have someone to talk it through with.

  5. S5

    Recognition that is real

    Good work gets noticed and acknowledged, within the team and in front of the people who matter.

Diversity, equity & inclusion

Good teams are made of different people who feel safe to speak up. These are the commitments we hold ourselves to.

  1. I1

    Respect for everyone

    People are treated with dignity, whatever their background, beliefs or role. Disrespect has no place in our team.

  2. I2

    Fair opportunity

    We aim to hire, reward and promote on skill and contribution, and to keep our processes open to anyone who is qualified. {# EDIT: only keep specifics you can prove #}

  3. I3

    A voice for every person

    Different perspectives make our work better. Everyone is encouraged to share ideas, concerns and feedback.

  4. I4

    Support when you need it

    If you need something to do your best work, tell us. We will work with you to find a reasonable way forward.
