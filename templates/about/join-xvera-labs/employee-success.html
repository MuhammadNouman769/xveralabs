What does the first 90 days look like?

You get a structured onboarding plan with a dedicated buddy, clear 30/60/90-day goals and regular check-ins with your manager so you ramp up with confidence.

How do promotions and career growth work?

We have transparent career levels for each role. Reviews happen twice a year, and your manager and mentor help you build a plan with the skills and impact needed for the next step.

Is there a learning budget?

Yes. Every team member gets a yearly learning budget for courses, certifications and conferences, plus paid time to study.

Do you support remote and flexible work?

We are remote-first. You can work from anywhere with flexible hours, as long as you collaborate well with your team and deliver on your goals.

What wellbeing benefits do you offer?

Health insurance, mental health support, wellness allowance and generous paid leave are available to every team member.
